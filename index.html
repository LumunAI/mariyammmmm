TODAY, WE CELEBRATE YOU

HAPPIEST BIRTHDAY
MARIYAMMMMMMM

A little reminder of how much beauty there is in being you.

There is something beautiful about a person who keeps finding room for joy, new dreams, and little moments that make life feel bright again.

Today, I don't want to celebrate any particular chapter. I want to celebrate you. The person you are, the kindness you carry, the little things that make you laugh, and all the lovely parts of you that make you wonderfully, unmistakably yourself.

You deserve happiness on ordinary days, too — peaceful mornings, unplanned laughter, people who appreciate you, and moments when you can simply be yourself without having to prove anything to anyone.

And there is something so special about the teacher in you. Every story you share, every lesson you make a little clearer, and every small dream you encourage can become part of a child's world for years to come. You help little minds discover how big their world can be.

I hope the care you give so freely finds its way back to you. I hope this year brings you gentler days, people who feel like sunshine, new things to look forward to, and a hundred unexpected reasons to smile.

Take your time with life. Try new things. Change your mind. Celebrate the small wins. Rest when you need to. You don't have to have everything figured out to enjoy the beautiful parts of the journey.

May you build a life that feels like your own — full of genuine laughter, quiet peace, freedom, wonder, and happiness that doesn't need a reason.

Your story is still unfolding, Mariyam. There are memories you haven't made yet, places you haven't seen, dreams you haven't thought of, and ordinary days that might turn out to be your favourites.

So today, let the wishes find you. Let yourself be celebrated. And may the year ahead bring beautiful things in ways you never expected.

With love, alwaysMD (Makkar MD) ♥
